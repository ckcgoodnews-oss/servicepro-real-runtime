## General Notes for All Images

- **Style:** Commercial marketing photography, NOT stock photo generic — should feel like a real local service company's hero image
- **Composition:** Leave space (roughly 1/3) for text overlay (company name, tagline, CTA button)
- **People:** Show competent, friendly professionals at work — diverse representation encouraged
- **Quality:** Sharp, well-lit, high resolution, professional color grading
- **Mood:** Trustworthy, competent, approachable, local-business friendly
- **Avoid:** Clip art style, cartoonish, overly staged/artificial poses, stock watermarks
- **Format:** Save as optimized JPG at 1200×600px, quality 85%, then update the `industryHeroMap` in `publicStorefront.js` to reference `.jpg` instead of `.svg`

## After Image Generation

Once all images are generated and saved to `apps/web/public/storefront/industries/`:

1. Update `apps/api/src/routes/publicStorefront.js` — change `.svg` → `.jpg` in the `industryHeroMap`
2. Optionally keep the SVG versions as fallbacks
3. Commit and push
