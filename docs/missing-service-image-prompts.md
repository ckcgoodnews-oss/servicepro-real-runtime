## General Notes

Apply the same pattern (3 existing + 4-7 additional common services) for ALL 30 industries. The additional services should represent the most commonly booked service types for each trade that a business owner would want to list on their public storefront.

### Naming Convention
- Filename: lowercase, hyphenated slug of the service name
- Location: `apps/web/public/storefront/services/{pack-code}/{slug}.jpg`
- Example: "AC Repair" → `pack-hvac/ac-repair.jpg`

### After Generation
Once images are created and placed in the correct folders, the service image dropdown in the Storefront Builder should be updated to show pack-specific service images (not just industry hero images). This requires a code update to scan the available images per pack and present them in the dropdown.
