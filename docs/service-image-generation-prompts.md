# ServicePro — Service Page Image Generation Prompts

> Generate one image per suggested service for each industry pack.
> Quality: Fortune 500 corporate marketing standard — clean, polished, family-friendly.
> Output: 800×500px landscape, JPG, quality 90%
> Save to: `apps/web/public/storefront/services/{industry}/{service-slug}.jpg`
> Style: Professional commercial photography, warm natural lighting, diverse representation, clean compositions, no text overlays.

---

## Global Style Guidelines

- **Quality level:** Fortune 500 annual report / enterprise SaaS marketing material
- **Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, welcoming, family-friendly
- **People:** Diverse ages, ethnicities, and genders. Friendly and competent. No overly posed stock-photo smiles.
- **Environment:** Clean, well-maintained, real-world settings (homes, businesses, outdoors)
- **Color:** Warm natural light, slight color grading toward each industry's accent color
- **Composition:** Subject in focus with context visible. Leave 20% breathing room for potential text overlay on the right side.
- **Avoid:** Anything unsafe, messy, disturbing, overtly branded, clip-art style, or AI-artifact heavy
