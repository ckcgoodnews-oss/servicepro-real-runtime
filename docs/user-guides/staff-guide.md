# Staff Guide

## Overview

As office staff, you handle the day-to-day customer interactions that keep the business running — creating customer records, preparing estimates, sending invoices, processing payments, and keeping the schedule organized. This guide walks you through everything you'll use daily.

---

## Creating and Managing Customers

### What It Is

The Customers section is your master list of everyone your business serves. Each customer record holds their contact details, job history, invoices, and communication log.

### Key Capabilities

- Add new customers with contact details and addresses
- Search and filter your customer list
- View a customer's complete history (past jobs, invoices, notes)
- Edit customer information
- Merge duplicate customer records

### How to Create a Customer

1. Click **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Customer**.
3. Enter their name, phone number, and email.
4. Add their service address (where work is performed).
5. Add any notes (gate code, pet info, preferred contact method).
6. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Always check if the customer already exists before creating a new one — use the search bar.
- Add a billing address if it's different from the service address.
- Tag customers (residential, commercial, VIP) to filter them easily later.

## Creating Estimates

### What It Is

An estimate is a quote you send to a customer before work begins. It lists the services, materials, and total cost so the customer can approve the work.

### Key Capabilities

- Create estimates with line items (services and materials)
- Apply discounts or taxes
- Send estimates to customers for approval
- Track which estimates have been approved, declined, or are pending
- Convert approved estimates directly into invoices

### How to Create an Estimate

1. Click **Estimates** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Estimate**.
3. Select the customer.
4. Add line items — choose services from your catalog or type custom entries.
5. Set quantities and prices.
6. Add any discounts or notes.
7. Click **Save** (to finish later) or **Send to Customer**.

### Tips

- Include a clear description of the work so the customer knows exactly what they're paying for.
- Set an expiration date on estimates to encourage timely decisions.
- You can duplicate a past estimate for a similar job to save time.

## Creating Invoices

### What It Is

Invoices are sent to customers after work is complete (or as agreed). They show what was done, what it costs, and how to pay.

### Key Capabilities

- Create invoices from scratch or from approved estimates
- Add line items with prices, quantities, and taxes
- Set payment terms and due dates
- Send invoices by email
- Track payment status (Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid)

### How to Create an Invoice

1. Click **Invoices** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Invoice**.
3. Select the customer.
4. Add line items (or click **Import from Estimate** to pull in an approved estimate).
5. Set the due date and payment terms.
6. Click **Save** or **Send to Customer**.

### Tips

- Invoices sent by email include a link where customers can pay online (if online payments are enabled).
- Mark partial payments when a customer pays in installments.
- Add notes to the invoice for special instructions or thank-you messages.

## Processing Payments

### What It Is

Record payments as they come in — whether by card, check, cash, or bank transfer. Keep your accounts balanced and give customers receipts.

### Key Capabilities

- Record full or partial payments against an invoice
- Accept multiple payment methods (cash, check, card, transfer)
- Send payment receipts automatically
- View payment history for any customer
- Process refunds when needed

### How to Record a Payment

1. Open the invoice you're recording payment for.
2. Click **Record Payment**.
3. Enter the amount received.
4. Select the payment method.
5. Add a reference number if applicable (check number, transaction ID).
6. Click **Save**.

A receipt is generated automatically and can be sent to the customer.

### Tips

- If a customer pays in full, the invoice status changes to **Paid** automatically.
- For recurring customers, you can set up automatic payment reminders for overdue invoices.
- Always record the payment method — it helps with reconciliation later.

## Scheduling Appointments

### What It Is

The Schedule page lets you book and manage appointments for customers. You can see who's available, find open time slots, and confirm bookings.

### Key Capabilities

- View the full team schedule (day, week, or month)
- Create new appointments
- Reschedule by dragging to a new time
- Cancel appointments and notify the customer
- Check for scheduling conflicts

### How to Book an Appointment

1. Click **Schedule** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Appointment** or click an empty time slot.
3. Select the customer.
4. Choose the service type.
5. Pick a date, time, and technician.
6. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Check the technician's existing schedule before booking to avoid double-booking.
- If you're not sure who to assign, leave the technician blank — a manager can assign from Dispatch later.
- Set up appointment confirmation emails to go out automatically.

## Managing the Knowledge Base

### What It Is

The Knowledge Base is your team's internal library of articles, guides, and how-to documents. Staff can create and organize articles that help the whole team.

### Key Capabilities

- Create articles with step-by-step instructions
- Organize articles into categories
- Search the knowledge base
- Mark articles as internal-only or customer-facing

### How to Add an Article

1. Go to **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Article**.
3. Enter a title and choose a category.
4. Write the content using the text editor.
5. Set visibility (team only or public).
6. Click **Publish**.

### Tips

- Document common questions customers ask so anyone on the team can answer consistently.
- Keep articles short and focused — one topic per article.
- Review and update articles quarterly to keep information current.

---

## Sending Notifications

### What It Is

Send messages to customers about their appointments, jobs, or invoices. Use templates for common messages or write custom ones.

### Key Capabilities

- Send email or text notifications to customers
- Use pre-built templates (appointment reminders, invoice notices, follow-ups)
- Customize messages with customer and job details
- View sent message history

### How to Send a Notification

1. Open the relevant record (customer, appointment, or invoice).
2. Click the **Notify** button or envelope icon.
3. Choose a template or write your message.
4. Select delivery method (email, text, or both).
5. Review and click **Send**.

### Tips

- Double-check the customer's contact details before sending, especially for new customers.
- Use templates for consistency — they automatically fill in names, dates, and job details.
- Check the message log if a customer says they didn't receive something.

---

## Viewing Reports

### What It Is

Reports help you track what's happening across the business. As staff, you can view customer reports, invoice summaries, and payment records.

### Key Capabilities

- View outstanding invoices and aging reports
- Track daily/weekly payment collections
- See customer activity (new sign-ups, repeat visits)
- Export reports as spreadsheets

### How to View a Report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the report type.
3. Set your date range and any filters.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click **Export** to download a copy if needed.

### Tips

- The "Aging Report" shows which invoices are overdue and by how long — great for follow-up calls.
- Export payment reports at the end of each day for your records.
- Use filters to narrow down to specific customers or time periods.
