# Storefront Builder Guide

## Overview

The Storefront Builder lets you create a public business page where potential customers can discover your services, learn about your company, and book appointments. Think of it as your mini-website — no design skills required.

---

## What the Storefront Is and How Customers See It

### What It Is

Your storefront is a public web page that anyone can visit. It displays your business name, description, services, and a way to get in touch or book. When someone searches for your business online or clicks a link you share, this is what they see.

### What Customers See

- Your business name and logo
- A cover image (hero image) at the top
- A description of what you do
- A list of your services with descriptions and pricing
- A way to request service or book an appointment
- Your contact information

---

## Setting Your Business Name and Description

### What It Is

Your business name and description are the first things visitors read. They should clearly explain who you are and what you do.

### How to Set Them

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Find the **Business Name** field at the top.
3. Enter your business name exactly as you want it to appear.
4. In the **Description** field, write 2-3 sentences explaining your services and what makes you different.
5. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Keep your description focused on what customers care about: what you do, where you serve, and why they should choose you.
- Example: "Johnson Plumbing provides fast, reliable plumbing repair and installation in the greater Portland area. Family-owned since 2005, we treat every home like our own."
- Avoid jargon — write as if explaining to a neighbor.

## Choosing a Theme

### What It Is

Themes control the colors and overall look of your storefront page. Pick one that matches your brand or the impression you want to make.

### How to Choose a Theme

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Theme** section.
3. Browse the available themes (each shows a preview).
4. Click the one you like.
5. The preview updates immediately so you can see how it looks.
6. Click **Save** when you're happy.

### Tips

- Pick a theme that's easy to read — high contrast between text and background.
- Lighter themes tend to work well for residential services; darker themes suit modern/tech brands.
- You can change your theme at any time without losing your content.

---

## Selecting a Cover Image (Hero Image)

### What It Is

The cover image (also called hero image) is the large banner photo at the top of your page. It sets the tone for visitors.

### How to Select a Cover Image

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Find the **Cover Image** section.
3. Click the **dropdown** to browse available images.
4. Select an image that represents your business (team photo, work in progress, clean results).
5. The preview updates instantly.
6. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Choose a high-quality image that's well-lit and professional.
- Images of real work (your team on a job, a finished project) build more trust than stock photos.
- The image will be cropped to fit the banner area — make sure the key content is in the center.
- If you don't see an image you like, ask your administrator to upload new options.

## Adding and Configuring Services

### What It Is

Services listed on your storefront tell visitors what you offer. Each service shows a name, description, and optionally a price or price range.

### How to Add Services

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Services** section.
3. Click **Add Service**.
4. Choose a service from your existing catalog, or create a new one.
5. Add or edit the public description (what customers see — keep it friendly and clear).
6. Optionally show a price or "Starting at" amount.
7. Click **Save**.

### How to Reorder Services

- Drag services up or down to change the display order.
- Put your most popular or important services at the top.

### Tips

- Limit your storefront to 6-10 services. Too many choices can overwhelm visitors.
- Use plain language in descriptions: "We'll fix your leaky faucet" beats "Faucet repair and replacement services."
- If you don't want to show prices publicly, leave the price field blank — customers will request a quote instead.

## Publishing and Previewing Your Page

### What It Is

Your storefront isn't visible to the public until you publish it. You can preview it at any time to see what customers will see.

### How to Preview

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Preview** in the top-right area.
3. A new tab opens showing your storefront as a visitor would see it.
4. Check that everything looks right — name, description, services, images.

### How to Publish

1. Make sure you're happy with the preview.
2. Go back to the Storefront Builder.
3. Click **Publish**.
4. Your page is now live and accessible at your public URL.

### How to Unpublish

- If you need to take your page down temporarily, click **Unpublish**. Your content is saved but hidden from the public.

### Tips

- Preview every time before publishing — especially after making changes.
- Share your storefront link on social media, email signatures, and business cards.
- Unpublishing doesn't delete anything — you can re-publish whenever you're ready.

## Editing Service Descriptions and Images

### What It Is

After adding services, you can update their descriptions and images at any time to keep things fresh and accurate.

### How to Edit

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Find the service in the Services section.
3. Click **Edit** (pencil icon) next to the service.
4. Update the description, price, or image.
5. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Update descriptions seasonally (e.g., "Fall furnace tune-ups now available").
- Add images to services when possible — services with photos get more clicks.
- Keep descriptions under 2-3 sentences — visitors skim, they don't read essays.

## Setting Your Public URL Slug

### What It Is

Your URL slug is the web address where people find your storefront. It's the part that comes after the main ServicePro address (e.g., `servicepro.com/your-business-name`).

### How to Set It

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Find the **URL Slug** field (usually near the top or in settings).
3. Enter your preferred slug — keep it short, lowercase, and use dashes instead of spaces.
4. The system checks if it's available.
5. If available, click **Save**.

### Examples of Good Slugs

- `johnson-plumbing`
- `cool-air-hvac`
- `sparkle-clean-services`

### Tips

- Use your business name or a recognizable abbreviation.
- Avoid numbers and special characters — keep it simple and memorable.
- Once set, try not to change it — people may have bookmarked or linked to your current address.
- Share your full storefront URL on business cards, social media, and email signatures.
