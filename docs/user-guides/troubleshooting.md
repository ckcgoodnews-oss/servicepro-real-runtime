# Troubleshooting

## Overview

Running into an issue? This guide covers the most common problems and how to solve them. Start with the section that matches your situation.

---

## "I Can't Log In"

### Possible Causes

- Wrong email or password
- Account not yet activated
- Account has been deactivated by an administrator

### Steps to Fix

1. **Check your email address** — Make sure you're using the exact email associated with your account (no typos).
2. **Reset your password** — Click **Forgot Password** on the login page. You'll receive an email with a link to set a new password.
3. **Check your inbox** — Look for the reset email in your inbox and spam/junk folder. It may take a few minutes to arrive.
4. **Try again** — Use the new password to log in.
5. **Still not working?** — Contact your business administrator. Your account may need to be activated or unlocked.

### Tips

- Passwords are case-sensitive — check that Caps Lock isn't on.
- If you've never logged in before, look for a welcome email with your activation instructions.
- If your company uses two-factor authentication, make sure you have access to your authenticator app.

## "I Don't See Certain Features"

### Why This Happens

ServicePro shows you only the features that match your access level. Different roles (Owner, Manager, Technician, Staff) see different menu items and capabilities.

### Steps to Fix

1. **Check your access level** — Go to your profile (click your icon in the top-right corner) and look under Account Settings.
2. **Compare with what you need** — If you're a Technician, you won't see Invoices or Settings. If you're Staff, you won't see Dispatch.
3. **Request access** — Ask your business owner or manager to change your access level if you need to use a feature not currently available to you.

### Access Level Quick Reference

| Role | Can See |
|------|---------|
| Owner | Everything |
| Manager | Jobs, Dispatch, Schedule, Customers, Reports, Inventory, Settings (limited) |
| Technician | Schedule, Jobs (assigned), Materials |
| Staff | Customers, Estimates, Invoices, Payments, Schedule, Reports |
| Read Only | Everything (view only, cannot edit) |

### Tips

- If a feature recently disappeared, your administrator may have changed your access level — reach out to them.
- Some features are tied to your business's subscription plan. If an entire module is missing for everyone, the plan may not include it.

## "My Storefront Isn't Showing"

### Possible Causes

- The storefront hasn't been published yet
- The URL slug isn't set
- The storefront module isn't enabled for your account

### Steps to Fix

1. Go to **Storefront Builder** in the sidebar.
2. Check the status indicator — does it say **Published** or **Draft**?
3. If it says Draft, click **Publish**.
4. Check that a **URL slug** is set — without it, there's no public address for your page.
5. After publishing, try visiting your storefront URL in a new browser tab.
6. If you still can't see it, clear your browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete) and try again.

### Tips

- It can take a few minutes for changes to appear after publishing.
- Make sure your storefront has at least a business name and one service listed — blank storefronts may not display.
- If "Storefront Builder" doesn't appear in your sidebar at all, ask your platform administrator to enable the module.

## "I'm Getting an Error"

### General Steps

1. **Note the error message** — Write down or screenshot any error text you see.
2. **Refresh the page** — Press F5 or click the refresh button in your browser. Many errors are temporary.
3. **Try again in a few minutes** — If the system is busy, waiting briefly often resolves the issue.
4. **Clear your browser cache** — Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, clear cached files, and reload.
5. **Try a different browser** — If the issue persists, try Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.
6. **Contact your administrator** — If none of the above works, share the error message and what you were doing when it occurred.

### Tips

- Errors that mention "session" or "expired" usually mean you need to log in again.
- If the same error happens every time you do a specific action, it's likely a system issue — report it.
- Include details when reporting: what page you were on, what you clicked, and the exact error text.

## "My Session Expired"

### Why This Happens

For security, ServicePro automatically logs you out after a period of inactivity. This protects your account if you leave your computer unattended.

### Steps to Fix

1. Click **Sign In** on the session expired screen.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You'll be returned to where you were (in most cases).

### Tips

- Sessions typically expire after 30-60 minutes of inactivity (your company may configure this differently).
- If you're frequently getting logged out, check that your browser isn't set to clear cookies automatically.
- Save your work regularly — unsaved changes in forms may be lost when a session expires.

## "I Can't Find a Customer or Job"

### Possible Causes

- Misspelled name in the search
- The record is in a different workspace
- The record hasn't been created yet
- You don't have access to that record

### Steps to Fix

1. **Use the search bar** at the top of the page — type the customer name, phone number, or job number.
2. **Try partial matches** — Search for just the last name or first few digits of a phone number.
3. **Check your filters** — If you have filters active (date range, status, technician), try clearing them.
4. **Check the workspace** — If you manage multiple locations, make sure you're in the correct workspace (use the Workspace Switcher in the top-left).
5. **Ask a colleague** — They may have created the record under a slightly different name.

### Tips

- Phone numbers and email addresses are often more reliable search terms than names (no spelling variations).
- The search looks across customer names, phone numbers, emails, and addresses.
- If a job was recently created, give it a few seconds to appear in search results.

## "Images Aren't Loading"

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection
- Browser cache needs clearing
- Image file format not supported
- Image file was too large to upload

### Steps to Fix

1. **Wait a moment** — Large images may take a few seconds to load on slower connections.
2. **Refresh the page** — Press F5 to reload.
3. **Clear your browser cache** — Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, select cached images and files, and clear.
4. **Check the image format** — ServicePro supports JPG, PNG, and WebP. Other formats (BMP, TIFF, GIF) may not display correctly.
5. **Check the file size** — Images larger than 10MB may fail to upload. Resize the image and try again.

### Tips

- For the best experience, use JPG or PNG images under 5MB.
- If uploading from your phone, the camera app usually saves in JPG format — that works perfectly.
- If images load on one device but not another, the issue is likely your browser cache — clearing it almost always fixes it.

## "The Page Is Slow"

### Possible Causes

- Your internet connection is slow
- You're viewing a page with a large amount of data
- Your subscription plan has usage limits that affect performance
- Your browser has too many tabs open

### Steps to Fix

1. **Check your internet** — Try loading another website to make sure your connection is working normally.
2. **Close extra tabs** — Too many open tabs can slow down your browser.
3. **Use filters** — If you're on a page with hundreds or thousands of records (Jobs, Customers), use filters to narrow the list.
4. **Refresh the page** — Sometimes a fresh load is faster than one that's been open for hours.
5. **Try a different browser** — Chrome and Edge typically offer the best performance with ServicePro.

### Plan Limitations

Some subscription plans have limits that may affect speed:
- **Record limits** — Lower-tier plans may have caps on how many customers or jobs you can store.
- **Storage limits** — If you've uploaded many photos and documents, you may be near your storage cap.
- **User limits** — Having more team members logged in simultaneously may affect responsiveness on smaller plans.

If you consistently experience slowness, talk to your business owner about upgrading the plan or contact your platform administrator.

### Tips

- Keep your browser updated to the latest version for best performance.
- Clear your browser cache once a month as general maintenance.
- Bookmark pages you visit frequently — navigating via bookmarks is faster than clicking through menus.
