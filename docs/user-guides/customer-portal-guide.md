# Customer Portal Guide

## Overview

The Customer Portal is your personal window into the services you receive. You can book appointments, view invoices, check on job progress, and communicate with your service team — all from one place, without needing to call or email.

---

## Logging In to Your Account

### What It Is

Your customer account gives you access to your service history, invoices, and the ability to book new appointments online.

### How to Log In

1. Go to the customer portal link provided by your service company (usually found on their website or in their emails).
2. Enter your **email address**.
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

### First-Time Login

If this is your first time, click **Create Account** or use the link in your welcome email. You'll need:
- The email address your service company has on file for you
- A password you choose (at least 8 characters)

### Tips

- If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password** and check your email for reset instructions.
- Bookmark the portal page for easy access next time.
- Some companies allow you to log in with just your email and a code sent to you — check for a **Magic Link** option.

## Booking a Service Appointment

### What It Is

Request a service visit without picking up the phone. Choose the service you need, pick a time that works, and your service team handles the rest.

### How to Book

1. Click **Book Appointment** or **Request Service** from your portal dashboard.
2. Choose the type of service you need from the list.
3. Add a description of the issue or what you need done.
4. Select your preferred date and time (available slots are shown).
5. Confirm your service address.
6. Click **Submit Request**.

You'll receive a confirmation email once the company reviews and schedules your appointment.

### Tips

- Be specific about the issue — "water leaking from under kitchen sink" helps more than "plumbing problem."
- If you're flexible on timing, mention it in the notes — you might get an earlier slot.
- You'll receive reminders before your appointment via email or text.

## Viewing Your Invoices and Making Payments

### What It Is

See all your invoices in one place — what you owe, what's been paid, and make payments online.

### Key Capabilities

- View all invoices (current and past)
- See itemized breakdowns of charges
- Make online payments by card or bank transfer
- Download invoices for your records
- View payment receipts

### How to Pay an Invoice

1. Click **Invoices** from your portal menu.
2. Find the invoice marked as **Due** or **Overdue**.
3. Click the invoice to view the details.
4. Click **Pay Now**.
5. Enter your payment details (card number or bank account).
6. Confirm the amount and click **Submit Payment**.

You'll receive a receipt by email immediately.

### Tips

- You can make partial payments if the full amount isn't possible right now.
- Click **Download** on any invoice to save a copy for your records or taxes.
- If you see a charge you don't recognize, click **Contact Us** to ask about it.

## Checking Appointment Status

### What It Is

Track where your appointment stands — from the moment you book until the job is complete.

### Status Stages

- **Requested** — You've submitted the request; the team is reviewing it.
- **Confirmed** — Your appointment is booked and a technician is assigned.
- **Technician En Route** — The technician is on their way to you.
- **In Progress** — Work is happening right now.
- **Completed** — The job is done.

### How to Check Status

1. Click **Appointments** or **My Services** from your portal menu.
2. Find the appointment in question.
3. The current status is shown next to each appointment.

### Tips

- You'll receive notifications as the status changes (if enabled by your service company).
- If your appointment says "Confirmed" but no one shows up, use the messaging feature to contact the team.
- Completed appointments include a summary of work done — review it to make sure everything looks right.

## Viewing Your Equipment and Asset Records

### What It Is

If your service company tracks your equipment (HVAC units, water heaters, appliances, etc.), you can view those records in your portal.

### Key Capabilities

- See all equipment registered to your address
- View make, model, and serial number
- Check maintenance history for each piece of equipment
- See warranty status and expiration dates
- View recommended service schedules

### How to View Your Equipment

1. Click **My Equipment** or **Assets** from your portal menu.
2. See the list of equipment at your address.
3. Click any item for full details and service history.

### Tips

- If you notice equipment missing from the list, let your service company know so they can add it.
- Keep an eye on warranty expiration dates — getting service done before warranties expire can save you money.
- Maintenance history is useful when selling your home or filing insurance claims.

## Sending Messages to the Service Team

### What It Is

Send questions or messages directly to your service company without calling. Great for non-urgent requests, following up on appointments, or asking about an invoice.

### How to Send a Message

1. Click **Messages** or **Contact Us** from your portal menu.
2. Click **New Message**.
3. Type your subject and message.
4. Attach a photo if helpful (e.g., a photo of the issue you need fixed).
5. Click **Send**.

### Tips

- For emergencies, always call the company directly — messages may not be read immediately.
- Include your address and any relevant details so the team can help you faster.
- Check back for replies — you'll also get an email notification when the team responds.

---

## Reviewing and Approving Estimates

### What It Is

When your service company prepares a quote for work that needs to be done, you'll see it in your portal for review and approval.

### How to Review an Estimate

1. Click **Estimates** from your portal menu (or click the link in the email notification).
2. Open the estimate to see the itemized list of services and costs.
3. Review each line item and the total.
4. If you're happy with it, click **Approve**.
5. If you have questions or want changes, click **Decline** or **Request Changes** and add a note explaining what you'd like changed.

### Tips

- Approved estimates typically turn into a scheduled appointment — you'll be contacted to pick a time.
- If the estimate has an expiration date, approve it before that date to lock in the pricing.
- You can approve part of an estimate if the company offers that option — just check the items you want done.
- Keep approved estimates for your records — they serve as a written agreement on scope and price.
