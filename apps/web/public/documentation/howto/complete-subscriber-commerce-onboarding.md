# HOW TO — Complete Subscriber Commerce Onboarding

1. Configure the organization name and portal branding.
2. Add at least one active service.
3. Open **Settings → Billing → Customer Payments** and deliberately choose whether to accept customer payments.
4. If enabled, connect Stripe, confirm charges, choose currency, and retain the supported card method.
5. Configure the Customer Portal.
6. Configure Express Service only when eligible services and service areas are ready.
7. Open **Settings → Billing → Commerce readiness** and resolve every blocking item.
8. Run a Stripe test-mode customer transaction.

Readiness is tenant scoped and resumable. It never enables customer payments automatically.
