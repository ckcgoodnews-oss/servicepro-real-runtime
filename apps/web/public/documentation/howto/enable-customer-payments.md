# Enable Customer Payments

ServicePRO subscription fees are paid to Aardvark Enterprises. Payments from your customers are processed through your connected Stripe account and paid to your business.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Open **Customer payments**.
3. Select **Connect Stripe** and complete Stripe's hosted identity and payout setup.
4. Return to **Settings → Customer Payments**. Charges must show **Enabled**.
5. Turn on **Accept Customer Payments**.
6. Select the currency, partial-payment, deposit, receipt, and reminder options you use.
7. Select **Save payment settings**.

The option remains unavailable if Stripe has requested more information or disabled charges.
