## Overview

This page provides quick step-by-step instructions for the most common things you'll do in ServicePro. Each task is kept short so you can follow along quickly.

---

## How to Create a Customer

1. Click **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Customer**.
3. Fill in the customer's name, phone, and email.
4. Add their service address.
5. Add any notes (gate code, pet info, preferred contact method).
6. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** Search for the customer first to avoid creating duplicates.

---

## How to Create a Work Order

1. Click **Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Work Order**.
3. Select the customer (or create a new one).
4. Choose the service type from the dropdown.
5. Add a description of the work needed.
6. Set the priority level (Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
7. Assign a technician (optional — can be done later from Dispatch).
8. Click **Create**.

**Tip:** Add detailed notes about the issue so the technician knows what to expect.

---

## How to Schedule an Appointment

1. Click **Schedule** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Appointment** or click an empty time slot on the calendar.
3. Select the customer.
4. Choose the service type.
5. Pick the date and time.
6. Assign a technician.
7. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** Check the technician's existing schedule to avoid double-booking.

---

## How to Create an Estimate

1. Click **Estimates** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Estimate**.
3. Select the customer.
4. Add line items — services and/or materials with quantities and prices.
5. Add notes or terms if needed.
6. Click **Save** to finish later, or **Send to Customer** to email it immediately.

**Tip:** Set an expiration date so customers know how long the pricing is valid.

---

## How to Convert an Estimate to an Invoice

1. Click **Estimates** in the sidebar.
2. Find the estimate that has been approved by the customer.
3. Open it and click **Convert to Invoice**.
4. Review the line items (make adjustments if the scope changed).
5. Set the due date and payment terms.
6. Click **Create Invoice**.

**Tip:** Only approved estimates can be converted. If the status still shows "Pending," follow up with the customer first.

---

## How to Record a Payment

1. Click **Invoices** in the sidebar.
2. Open the invoice you're recording payment for.
3. Click **Record Payment**.
4. Enter the amount received.
5. Select the payment method (cash, check, card, transfer).
6. Add a reference number if applicable.
7. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** For partial payments, enter only the amount received — the invoice will show the remaining balance.

---

## How to Add Inventory Items

1. Click **Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Item**.
3. Enter the item name, description, and category.
4. Set the unit cost and current quantity in stock.
5. Set a reorder threshold (you'll get an alert when stock drops below this number).
6. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** Use consistent naming conventions so everyone on the team can find items quickly.

---

## How to Send a Notification

1. Open the relevant record (customer, appointment, job, or invoice).
2. Click the **Notify** button or envelope icon.
3. Choose a message template or write a custom message.
4. Select the delivery method — email, text message, or both.
5. Click **Send**.

**Tip:** Use templates for consistency. They automatically fill in customer names, dates, and details.

---

## How to Export Data

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the report type you want to export (customers, jobs, invoices, etc.).
3. Set your date range and any filters.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click **Export** in the top-right corner.
6. Choose your format (spreadsheet is the most common).
7. The file downloads to your computer.

**Tip:** Exports include all columns visible in the report. Adjust filters before exporting to get only the data you need.

---

## How to Change Your Password

1. Click your **profile icon** in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Account Settings**.
3. Click **Change Password**.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Enter your new password (at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers).
6. Confirm your new password.
7. Click **Save**.

**Tip:** Use a unique password you don't use on other websites. Consider using a password manager.

---

## How to Enable Two-Factor Authentication

1. Click your **profile icon** in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Account Settings**.
3. Find the **Security** section.
4. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
5. Open your authenticator app on your phone (like Google Authenticator or Authy).
6. Scan the QR code shown on screen.
7. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to confirm.
8. Save the backup codes in a secure place (in case you lose access to your phone).
9. Click **Done**.

**Tip:** Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of security. Even if someone guesses your password, they can't get in without your phone.
