## What Is ServicePro?

ServicePro is an all-in-one platform that helps service businesses manage their daily operations — from scheduling jobs and dispatching technicians to invoicing customers and tracking performance. Everything your team needs lives in one place, accessible from any computer or mobile device.

---

## Logging In for the First Time

### Overview

When your account is created, you'll receive an email with your login details. Here's how to sign in.

### Steps

1. Open your web browser and go to your company's ServicePro address (your admin will share this link with you).
2. Enter your **email address** in the Email field.
3. Enter your **password** in the Password field.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. If this is your first login, you may be asked to set a new password. Choose something you'll remember — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

### Tips

- Bookmark the login page so you can find it quickly next time.
- If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your email.
- If two-factor authentication is enabled for your account, you'll need to enter a code from your authenticator app after your password.

## Navigating the Main Screen

### Overview

Once logged in, you'll see three main areas on every page. Understanding these will help you find anything quickly.

### Key Areas

- **Sidebar (left side)** — Your main menu. Click any item to go to that section: Dashboard, Customers, Jobs, Dispatch, Estimates, Invoices, Inventory, Reports, and Settings. The items you see depend on your access level.

- **Top Bar (across the top)** — Shows your company name, search bar, notifications bell, and your profile icon. Click the bell to see alerts. Click your profile icon to access your account settings or log out.

- **Workspace Switcher** — If you manage multiple business locations, you'll see a dropdown in the top-left corner that lets you switch between workspaces. Each workspace has its own customers, jobs, and settings.

### Tips

- The sidebar can be collapsed by clicking the menu icon (three lines) at the top to give you more screen space.
- Use the **search bar** in the top bar to quickly find customers, jobs, or invoices by name or number.
- Look for the blue dot on the notifications bell — that means you have unread alerts.

## Understanding Your Access Level

### Overview

Not everyone in your company needs access to everything. ServicePro uses access levels to make sure each person sees only what they need.

### Access Levels

- **Owner** — Full access to everything. Can manage team members, view all reports, change business settings, and configure the platform.

- **Manager** — Can view and manage most features including dispatch, scheduling, work orders, and reports. Cannot delete records or change core business settings.

- **Technician** — Focused on field work. Can view their schedule, update job statuses, log materials, and add notes. Cannot access billing or business settings.

- **Billing/Staff** — Handles customer records, estimates, invoices, and payments. Can view reports and export data.

- **Read Only** — Can view information but cannot make changes. Useful for stakeholders who need visibility without editing.

### Tips

- If you can't see a menu item or button, it's likely because your access level doesn't include it. Ask your business owner or manager to adjust your access if needed.
- Your access level is shown on your profile page under **Account Settings**.

## Getting Help

### Overview

ServicePro offers several ways to get help when you're stuck.

### Options

- **In-App Help** — Click the question mark icon (?) in the top-right corner of any page for tips specific to that screen.
- **Knowledge Base** — If your company has set up the Knowledge Base, you'll find articles and how-to guides in the sidebar.
- **Your Administrator** — For access issues or account changes, contact your business owner or manager directly.
- **Search** — Use the search bar to find customers, jobs, or settings by typing a keyword.

### Tips

- Most pages include tooltips — hover over icons or labels to see a short explanation.
- If something isn't working as expected, try refreshing your browser first.
- Check the [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md) for solutions to common issues.
