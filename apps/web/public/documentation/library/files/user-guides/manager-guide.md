## Overview

As an operations manager, you're the person keeping everything running smoothly. You assign technicians to jobs, manage the daily schedule, monitor team performance, and make sure work gets done on time. This guide covers everything you can do in ServicePro.

---

## Dispatch Board

### What It Is

The Dispatch board is your command center for assigning technicians to jobs. It shows all unassigned work orders and your team's availability in one view.

### Key Capabilities

- See all unassigned jobs at a glance
- View each technician's current workload and location
- Drag and drop jobs onto technicians (or assign manually)
- Filter by date, priority, or service type
- See real-time status updates as technicians move through their day

### How to Assign a Job

1. Click **Dispatch** in the sidebar.
2. Find the unassigned job in the left panel.
3. Review the job details (location, service type, estimated duration).
4. Either drag it onto a technician's timeline, or click the job and select **Assign** then choose a technician.
5. The technician will receive a notification immediately.

### Tips

- Use the map view to assign jobs based on proximity — save your techs driving time.
- Color coding shows priority: red for urgent, yellow for high, green for routine.
- If a technician calls in sick, reassign their jobs by dragging them to another team member.

## Scheduling Appointments

### What It Is

The Schedule page shows all booked appointments in a calendar view. You can create new appointments, reschedule existing ones, and manage time slots.

### Key Capabilities

- View schedule by day, week, or month
- Create new appointments for customers
- Reschedule by dragging appointments to a new time
- Block out unavailable times (holidays, breaks, meetings)
- Filter by technician or service type

### How to Schedule an Appointment

1. Click **Schedule** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **+ New Appointment** button (or click directly on an empty time slot).
3. Select the customer (or create a new one).
4. Choose the service needed.
5. Pick the date and time.
6. Assign a technician.
7. Click **Save**.

The customer will receive a confirmation notification if notifications are enabled.

### Tips

- Double-click an existing appointment to edit its details.
- Use the week view for a quick picture of who's doing what.
- Check the technician's existing schedule before booking to avoid conflicts.

## Managing Work Orders

### What It Is

Work orders are the core of your daily operations. Each one represents a job that needs to be done — from the initial request through completion.

### Key Capabilities

- Create new work orders from scratch or from customer requests
- Update status (Open, In Progress, On Hold, Completed, Cancelled)
- Add notes, attachments, and checklists
- Link work orders to estimates and invoices
- Track time spent on each job

### How to Create a Work Order

1. Click **Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Work Order**.
3. Select the customer.
4. Choose the service type.
5. Add a description of the work needed.
6. Set priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
7. Assign a technician (optional — you can do this later from Dispatch).
8. Click **Create**.

### How to Update a Work Order

1. Click **Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. Find and click the work order you want to update.
3. Change the status, add notes, or update any field.
4. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- Use the filter and search to quickly find work orders by status, customer, or technician.
- Add internal notes (visible only to your team) and customer-facing notes separately.
- Link related work orders together when a job requires multiple visits.

## Viewing Team Performance

### What It Is

Track how your team is performing across key metrics so you can identify top performers and areas for improvement.

### Key Capabilities

- Jobs completed per technician (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Average job duration
- On-time arrival rates
- Customer satisfaction scores (if surveys are enabled)
- Revenue generated per technician

### How to View Performance

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Team Performance**.
3. Choose a date range.
4. View individual or team-wide metrics.

### Tips

- Use these reports in team meetings to celebrate wins and address issues.
- Compare performance month over month to spot trends.
- High performers may be candidates for more complex jobs or mentoring roles.

## Running Reports

### What It Is

Reports provide insights into every part of your operation — revenue, jobs, customers, inventory, and more.

### Key Capabilities

- Revenue reports (by service, by technician, by time period)
- Job reports (completion rates, average duration, status breakdown)
- Customer reports (new customers, repeat business, outstanding balances)
- Inventory reports (stock levels, usage, reorder alerts)
- Export any report to a spreadsheet

### How to Run a Report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the report category.
3. Set your filters (date range, technician, service type).
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click **Export** to download a copy.

### Tips

- Save frequently-used report configurations as favorites for quick access.
- Schedule recurring reports to be emailed to you automatically.
- Use date comparisons to identify growth or declining areas.

## Managing Inventory

### What It Is

Keep track of parts, materials, and supplies your team uses on the job. Know what's in stock, what's running low, and what technicians are using.

### Key Capabilities

- View current stock levels
- Add new inventory items
- Set reorder thresholds (get alerts when stock is low)
- Track which materials were used on each job
- Record incoming deliveries

### How to Add an Inventory Item

1. Click **Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Item**.
3. Enter the item name, description, and unit cost.
4. Set the current quantity and reorder threshold.
5. Click **Save**.

### Tips

- When technicians log materials on a job, inventory counts update automatically.
- Review the reorder alerts weekly to avoid running out of common parts.
- Use categories to organize items (electrical, plumbing, general supplies).

## Handling Customer Communications

### What It Is

Manage all outgoing notifications and messages to customers — appointment reminders, job updates, invoice notices, and follow-ups.

### Key Capabilities

- Send appointment confirmation and reminder emails/texts
- Notify customers when a technician is en route
- Send invoice and payment receipt notifications
- Create custom message templates
- View communication history for each customer

### How to Send a Notification

1. Open the relevant record (job, appointment, or invoice).
2. Click **Send Notification** or the envelope icon.
3. Choose a template or write a custom message.
4. Select the delivery method (email, text, or both).
5. Click **Send**.

### Tips

- Set up automatic notifications for common events (appointment booked, technician on the way, invoice ready) to save time.
- Personalize templates with the customer's name and job details.
- Check the communication log on a customer's profile to see what they've already received.

## Workflow Automation

### What It Is

Workflow automation lets you set up actions that happen automatically when certain things occur — saving you repetitive work.

### Key Capabilities

- Automatically assign jobs based on service type or territory
- Send notifications when a job status changes
- Create follow-up tasks when a job is completed
- Escalate overdue jobs to managers
- Generate invoices when jobs are marked complete

### How to Create an Automation

1. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Automations** tab.
3. Click **New Automation**.
4. Choose a trigger (e.g., "When a job is marked complete").
5. Choose an action (e.g., "Create an invoice").
6. Set any conditions or filters.
7. Click **Save and Activate**.

### Tips

- Start simple — automate one common task and see how it works before building complex chains.
- Test automations with a sample job before relying on them fully.
- You can pause an automation at any time without deleting it.
- Review your active automations monthly to make sure they still match your process.
