## Starting Your Trial

1. Go to the ServicePro website
2. Click **Start free** (top-right corner)
3. Enter your name, email, and create a password
4. Click **Continue** and enter your company name and industry
5. Click **Start free trial**
6. Check your email for a verification link (in development, you'll be logged in automatically)

Your trial lasts **14 days** and includes full access to ServicePro Professional — no credit card required.

## First Steps (Setup Checklist)

After logging in, you'll see a setup checklist on your dashboard. Work through these steps to get the most from your trial:

### 1. Add company information
Go to Settings and fill in your business name, address, phone number, and logo.

### 2. Configure business hours
Set your operating days and hours so scheduling works correctly.

### 3. Select your services
Add the services you offer (plumbing repair, AC maintenance, lawn care, etc.).

### 4. Add or invite team members
Invite technicians or office staff. Each gets their own login.

### 5. Add your first customer
Create a customer record with their name, phone, email, and address.

### 6. Create your first work order
Create a job, assign a technician, and walk through the lifecycle (scheduled → in progress → completed).

### 7. Preview your customer portal
See what your customers see when they log in to check appointments and invoices.

### 8. Publish or preview your storefront
Build a simple website where customers can request service online.

## The Core Workflow

ServicePro connects your daily operations in one place:

```
Customer books service
     ↓
Appointment is scheduled
     ↓
Technician is dispatched
     ↓
Work order is created and tracked
     ↓
Estimate is generated (if needed)
     ↓
Invoice is sent
     ↓
Payment is recorded
     ↓
Report shows revenue and performance
```

## Trial Status

You can always see how many days remain in your trial from the banner at the top of the dashboard.

When your trial ends:
- Your data is preserved (nothing is deleted)
- Editing and operational actions are paused
- You can still sign in and view your data
- You can upgrade at any time to resume full access
- You can export your data if you decide not to continue

## Upgrading

Ready to keep using ServicePro? Click **View Plans** in the trial banner or go to Settings → Upgrade.

Plans start at **$49/month** for small teams. Our sales team can help you find the right fit — just click **Contact Sales** in the upgrade page.

All your trial data (customers, jobs, invoices, settings, storefront) is preserved when you upgrade. Nothing to re-enter.

## FAQ

**Do I need a credit card to start?**
No. The trial is completely free for 14 days.

**What happens when the trial expires?**
Your data stays safe. You can sign in, view data, and export it. To resume full operations, choose a paid plan.

**Can I extend my trial?**
Contact our team and we can extend your trial if you need more time.

**Can I use my own domain for the storefront?**
During trial, your storefront uses a ServicePro subdomain. Custom domains are available on paid plans.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Each trial workspace is completely isolated. No other user or tenant can see your data.
